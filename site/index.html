Ponds, water features & koi

From a dug hole to clear water and healthy fish

The specialty since 2021: new builds, rescues of ponds that have gone green, koi care, and the yearly upkeep that keeps fish healthy and pumps running.

  • Pond builds and remodelsExcavation, liner or preformed shell, rock work, plants and fish-safe startup.
  • Waterfalls, streams and fountainsRecirculating features sized to the pump, so the water moves without overflowing.
  • Koi ponds and fish careKoi-depth builds and filtration, water testing, quarantine for new fish, and moving fish safely during work.
  • CleanoutsDrain, remove sludge and leaves, rinse rock, refill with dechlorinated water. Fish held safely.
  • Pumps, filters and UVDiagnose, repair or replace. Sized by gallons and head height, not guesswork.
  • Leak finding and liner repairTrack down where the water goes, patch or reline.
  • Seasonal open and closeSpring startup, fall netting, and keeping an opening in the ice on cold snaps.

What your pond needs, month by month

Spring startup Mar–Apr
Cleanout, restart pumps and filters, check the liner after winter, start feeding fish again once the water passes 50°F.
Planting May–Jun
Add plants for shade and filtration, set up the UV, and balance the water before the heat arrives.
Summer heat Jul–Sep
Top off for evaporation, keep algae down, and add aeration: warm water holds less oxygen for fish.
Leaf season Oct–Nov
Net the pond before the oaks and maples drop, empty skimmers often, and cut back plants.
Winter Dec–Feb
Shut down or run a small pump, keep a hole open in the ice on freezing nights, and plan repairs and new builds.
